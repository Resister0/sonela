Sonela makes free, open-source apps for PC gamers, made by Resister0, one independent developer. The apps are native, do one job each, and stay light on your PC while you play.

Free and open source

The apps are free, with no ads and no tracking. Echo's code is public on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 licence, and this website's code is there too (MIT licence). Echo Cloud, Echo's optional clip storage, has paid plans for people who want more space; that is what pays for the servers.
