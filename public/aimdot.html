Sonela Aimdot · Crosshair overlay

Aimdot

Your own crosshair on top of any game. Design it or paint it, give each game its own, and turn it on or off with a hotkey.

Windows (installer)Coming soon Windows (portable zip)Coming soon Linux (AppImage)Coming soon Linux (tar.gz)Coming soon

Get news on Discord

Free and open source. No ads, no account.

Design it

Pick the shape, colour, size, gap and outline, and watch it change as you go.

Or paint it

Draw your crosshair pixel by pixel, with layers and animation.

Use a picture

Any image of your own can be your crosshair.

One per game

Give each game its own crosshair. Aimdot switches when the game starts.

Share codes

Send your crosshair to a friend as a short code, or bring yours over from CS2 or Valorant.

Hotkeys

Show, hide or switch crosshairs without leaving the game.

Out of the way

Lives in the tray and can start with your PC.

Game Bar mode

On Windows, it also shows over games in exclusive fullscreen.

Very light

A small native app. You won't notice it while you play.
