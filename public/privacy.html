Privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This website collects nothing about you. It has no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads, no embedded videos or fonts from other sites, and no forms. It is plain pages.

The pages are served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare needs your IP address to send you the page, and may keep it in short-lived logs to keep the service running and safe. We only see total numbers (for example how many requests the site got in a day), never anything about one visitor.

Links to other sites (echoclip.link, GitHub, Discord) take you to those sites, and their own privacy policies apply there.

The apps

Each app has its own privacy policy, on its own website. Echo's is at echoclip.link/privacy.
