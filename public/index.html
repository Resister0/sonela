Crosshair overlay · out now

Aimdot puts your own crosshair on any game

Design it or paint it pixel by pixel, give each game its own, and show or hide it with a hotkey.

  • Over 100 presets, or your own picture
  • Share a crosshair as a short code, or bring yours from CS2 or Valorant
  • Game Bar mode for exclusive fullscreen on Windows

Light on your PC

No ads, no tracking and no paid version of the apps. Echo's optional cloud storage has paid plans for more space.

Open source

The code is on GitHub, so anyone can check what the apps do.

Windows and Linux

Windows 10 and 11, and Linux, including Steam Deck and Bazzite through Flatpak.
